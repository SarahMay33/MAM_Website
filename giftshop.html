The Manitoba Agricultural Museum gift shop is open year-round to visitors.

The gift shop stocks branded products of the Manitoba Agricultural Museum, selections of books, decorations, toys and clothing. It also stocks some necessity items for campers.

The Manitoba Agricultural Museum, in partnership with Samtronics, also produces every year DVDs focusing on the Manitoba Threshermen's Reunion & Stampede or specific themes.

Please contact the museum office for information on other available products.
