HOURS AND LOCATION

The Manitoba Agricultural Museum is open to the public year round

SUMMER HOURS
MUSEUM HOURS:
Everyday until September 30, 10 AM - 4 PM

OFFICE HOURS:
Monday - Friday, 8:30 AM - 4 PM


ADMISSION RATES:
Adults - $10
Seniors (65+) - $8
Children (6-18) - $8
Children (5 and under) - FREE
Family (2 adults + up to 4 kids under 18) - $30

These rates are for regular Museum visits. They DO NOT apply to museum special events including the Manitoba Threshermen's Reunion and Stampede. Please visit the events pages for applicable rates.
